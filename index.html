Quick Start

  1. Choose the family member helping with the decision.
  2. Spin the wheel.
  3. Review the timed activities in your suggested two-hour plan.
  4. Spin again if the plan does not fit tonight.
  5. Add a custom activity from the Activities tab if needed.

What the prototype does

SpinFamily uses the wheel's landed category and curated activity estimates to create a two-hour plan starting at the current device time. Chores lead into other activities, with bedtime last when included. The app does not check your real calendar or learn private household preferences.

What comes next

Future work may add validated fixed-event constraints, caregiver-controlled routines, history, and private household preferences without changing the familiar wheel.

About SpinFamily

SpinFamily is designed to reduce family evening decision fatigue through a quick, participatory planning experience.

Current prototype capabilities

  • Four curated activity categories
  • Category-led two-hour evening schedules with explicit estimated activity times
  • Session-only custom activities
  • Basic client-side family-friendly text screening for custom activity names

Free accounts · Saved family progress

Anonymous spins and two-hour suggestions are free. Signed-in Free and Plus households can save selected plans, mark them complete explicitly, revisit their history, and remove records. A selection never counts as a completion; progress is derived only from caregiver-confirmed completions.

Not implemented in this version

  • Machine-learning recommendations
  • Personalized member scoring
  • Full caregiver-managed member profiles
  • Weather-aware suggestions
  • Validated activity-by-activity progress and variety analytics

Prototype status: public beta/proof of execution. Public deployment does not represent production security, privacy, or ML approval.
