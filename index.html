SpinFamily is a Rootlayer family evening-planning prototype focused on reducing nightly decision friction with a playful but explainable planning experience.
Cloud accounts and cross-device synchronization are not implemented in this version.
The vNext foundation is parent-managed and local-first. Child-owned accounts and unnecessary child-identifying data are out of scope.
