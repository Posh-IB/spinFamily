Our story

It started with one hardworking mother.

She wanted more meaningful time with her children after work. But after a full day of making decisions, planning the evening felt like a second job.

She tried an early version of SpinFamily to plan one evening. That conversation became the company. Today we’re building it with families, one real evening at a time.
